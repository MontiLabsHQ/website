# Progressus Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what the Progressus app does with your data. It is published at
https://montilabs.co/progressus/privacy. Progressus is made by Monti Labs ("we", "us"). You can
reach us at info@montilabs.co.

## In short

- Progressus has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. Your progress lives on your iPhone
  and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage, which we cannot read.
- The numbers Progressus reads from Apple Health stay on your iPhone. They never reach iCloud, us
  or anyone else.
- We do not sell data, show ads or track you across apps and websites.
- Two services help us run the app: TelemetryDeck counts anonymous usage events, and RevenueCat
  checks whether your Premium subscription is active. Neither learns who you are.

## Data on your iPhone and in your iCloud

Progressus stores your answers from the first questions (as the options you chose), your quests,
their daily progress, your days, XP, level, rank, class, gates, trials, titles and settings. This
data is kept in the app's storage on your iPhone. The Progressus widgets read the same storage on
the same iPhone.

If iCloud is on for Progressus, this data is also copied to the private database of your iCloud
account (Apple CloudKit), so your other devices signed in to the same iCloud account see the same
progress. Only you can read that database; we have no access to it. You can stop the copy in the
iOS Settings app under your name, iCloud, and turn Progressus off. Progressus then keeps working
with the data on the iPhone alone.

## Apple Health

Progressus asks for Apple Health access only if you choose to connect it, and Apple Health
verification is part of Premium. You can say no and use the app fully without it.

With your permission Progressus reads, and never writes:

- steps,
- workouts and their type,
- sleep,
- mindful minutes,
- water.

What Progressus does with these readings:

- It completes the quests you link to Apple Health (for example "Walk 8,000 steps") and shows
  their progress. A quest done this way earns 1.25 times the XP.
- During the first questions it can read the averages of the last 14 days to suggest starting
  targets. Each average is rounded to the nearest answer option (for example "5,000 steps a day"),
  and only that option is saved, as if you had chosen it yourself.
- It counts your steps and workouts toward the week's gate while the app is open.

What stays where:

- The numbers read from Apple Health (steps, minutes, hours, liters, workouts) are held only in
  the iPhone's memory while Progressus runs. They are not saved by Progressus, they are not copied
  to iCloud, and they are never sent to us or to any other service.
- The only Health-related facts Progressus saves with your progress are your answer choices from
  the first questions, that a quest was completed and that Apple Health completed it. Like your
  other progress, these facts are copied to your private iCloud database if iCloud is on. They
  contain no measurement.
- On the iPhone only, Progressus also remembers whether it could read Apple Health the last time
  it checked, so it picks weekly gates your program can clear. This yes-or-no stays on the device
  and is not copied to iCloud.
- Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold and never shared with
  third parties, including TelemetryDeck and RevenueCat.

You can change or remove the access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security,
Health, Progressus. You then mark the quests linked to Apple Health by hand.

## Usage analytics (TelemetryDeck)

To learn which parts of Progressus help people, the app sends a few anonymous events to
TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany): for example that the first questions were finished, a
quest was completed, a day succeeded, a gate was cleared, the paywall was shown or closed, or a
subscription or trial started. An event can carry the number of the onboarding screen, a plan name
(annual or monthly), a rank letter or whether Hard Mode was turned on. Events never carry a quest
title, an answer, anything you typed or any Apple Health number. By default TelemetryDeck's software
adds to every event the device model and screen size, the operating system and app version, the
language, region and time zone, display and accessibility settings (such as dark mode or larger
text), the day of the week and the hour, and how many sessions and days you have used the app and
how long the sessions lasted, and it sends events of its own when a session starts and when the
app is opened for the first time.

Each event comes with an identifier that is a salted hash created on your iPhone. It cannot be
turned back into your device or your identity, and it is not linked to you. TelemetryDeck's own
policy is at https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/.

## Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Apple handles payment for Premium; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat (RevenueCat,
Inc., USA) receives the purchase records of your App Store transactions for Progressus with an
anonymous identifier it creates, so the app can tell whether Premium is active and restore it on
your other devices. Like any online service, RevenueCat's servers also see your device's IP
address and the app version when the app contacts them. We do not give RevenueCat your name, email
address or any Health data. RevenueCat's policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

## Notifications

Progressus schedules its reminders on your iPhone. No reminder passes through a server of ours.
iCloud may wake the app in the background so your progress syncs; that goes through Apple.

## Children

Progressus collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress stays until you delete it. Deleting the app removes the data on that iPhone. To
remove the copy in iCloud, open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage
(or Manage Storage), Progressus, and delete its data. TelemetryDeck and RevenueCat keep their
records under their own policies; because those records are not linked to you, we cannot single
yours out.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you have the
right to ask about, correct or delete personal data about you. We hold no data that identifies
you, but you are welcome to write to info@montilabs.co with any question.
